Frequently Answered Inquiries

Clear clarity on minimum quantities, financial instruments, lead schedules, and handling processes.

Typically, our minimum sea freight consignment is one standard 40ft refrigerated container (approx. 20 to 24 metric tons depending on produce density and packaging format). For urgent, premium, or high-value specialty shipments, we routinely dispatch consolidated air pallets directly from Cairo International Airport.
We adhere strictly to standard international trade practices. We accommodate Confirmed Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C) at sight issued by tier-one international banks, as well as milestone-structured Wire Transfer (T/T) arrangements established with your designated Crest trade director.
Orders are customarily fulfilled within 3 to 7 business days from order confirmation to reefer container stuffing. Lead schedules strictly respect active harvest pick windows, mandatory pre-cooling pulping cycles, accredited pesticide residue screening, and phytosanitary verification.

We operate multiple high-speed packing lines equipped for open-top 15kg cartons, 4.5kg - 5kg telescopic fruit boxes, retail punnets, heat-sealed carry bags with EAN barcodes, sturdy bulk wooden bins, and specialized plastic honeycomb trays for sensitive crops.
We sustain weekly direct sailings and air lift to premier receivers across Western & Southern Europe (Netherlands, Germany, United Kingdom, Italy, Spain), the GCC nations (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Oman), the Indian Subcontinent, and North America.
